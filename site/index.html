RYAN BARRINEAU'S

MOON MISSION

Land the lunar module on the Moon. Pick a site from orbit, ride the braking burn down, then fly the lander onto a pad with the main engine and the side jets. A GW-BASIC game for the IBM PC/XT and compatibles with CGA graphics, first written for a VTech Laser XT.

THE MISSION

ORBIT

Pick your landing site

Drift over the Moon and choose Tranquility Base, Fra Mauro or Hadley Rille: easy, medium or hard.

DESCENT

Fly it down

Throttle the main engine and steer with the side jets. Watch your speed and your fuel, and find level ground.

TOUCHDOWN

See it from the cockpit

A safe landing replays the last seconds of your own descent out of the commander's window.

3 LANDERS

Chase the high score

Narrow pads pay up to ten times. The top ten scores are saved on your START disk.

SCREENS

WHAT YOU NEED

  • An IBM PC, XT, AT or compatible (8088 or better) with CGA graphics. A Tandy 1000 works too. Or the free DOSBox emulator on a modern computer.
  • 512K of memory. With less, the title Moon stands still and the touchdown replay is skipped.
  • MS-DOS or PC-DOS 3.0 or later.
  • Your own copy of GW-BASIC. Version 3.22 is tested; Tandy's BASIC 2.02 runs it too.
  • Two 360K floppy drives, or one (the game asks you to swap disks), or a hard disk.
  • Not included: DOS and GW-BASIC. The download has the game only.

INSTALL

FLOPPIES
  1. Copy your GWBASIC.EXE onto the START disk.
  2. Boot your computer into DOS.
  3. Put START in A: and LUNAR LANDING in B:.
  4. Type:
A:
GO

One drive? Use A: for both; the game tells you when to swap. Leave START's write-protect tab off so high scores can be saved.

BOOT STRAIGHT IN

Run MAKEBOOT once from DOS. It puts your DOS, COMMAND.COM and GW-BASIC on the START disk, so it boots right into the game:

B:MAKEBOOT B: C:\DOS\GWBASIC.EXE

The first part is the START disk's drive, the second is where your GWBASIC.EXE is. If SYS says there's no room, format a blank disk with FORMAT B: /S, copy START onto it and run MAKEBOOT there.

HARD DISK OR DOSBOX

Copy the MOON folder to your hard disk and put GWBASIC.EXE in it, then:

CD \MOON
GO

In DOSBox, set machine=cga and about cycles=300 for real XT speed, then:

MOUNT C C:\GAMES
C:
CD \MOON
GO

The arrow keys work too. In orbit, 4 and 6 drift left and right and 2 or Enter descends. A gameport joystick or gamepad works as well: press J on the title screen to set it up, and answer Y to Hold A to burn if you want the engine to fire only while A is held.

THE GAUGES

ALT 120 H +1.5 V -3.2 THR 60
ALTHeight above the ground
HSideways speed: +1.5 means moving right
VUp/down speed: -3.2 means moving down
THRMain engine power, in percent
FuelThe bar at the top left
A safe landing: V no faster than -4.0, H within 2.0, and both feet on level ground.
